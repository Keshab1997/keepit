## Full description (≤4000)

```
Tired of saving reels, videos and articles… and never looking at them again?

KeepIt is your visual second brain. Save anything from any app in one tap, see it in a
beautiful visual feed, and let smart reminders bring the best ideas back to you at the right
time.

⚡ SAVE IN ONE TAP
Share from Instagram, YouTube, X, Chrome, or any app → KeepIt. The link becomes a rich card with
thumbnail, title and author automatically. Quick notes too.

🎨 A VISUAL FEED YOU'LL LOVE
A clean masonry grid of everything you saved. Instant search, smart auto-tags and Spaces
(Videos, Reels, Articles, Notes, Top of Mind) keep it organised without effort.

🔔 SERENDIPITY: NEVER FORGET WHAT YOU SAVE
Spaced-repetition reminders resurface saved gems on day 3, 14, 45 and 90, plus a relaxing Sunday
digest. Mark as watched or snooze for a week right from the notification. You choose the time.

☁️ OPTIONAL BACKUP & SYNC
Sign in with Google to back up your mind to the cloud and sync across devices. Or don't:
KeepIt works fully offline with no account.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Local-first: your data lives on your phone
• Light, non-personalized ads (Google AdMob) keep KeepIt free — your data is never sold
• Export everything as JSON anytime
• Delete your account and data in one tap

PERFECT FOR
Creators collecting inspiration · Students saving lectures · Developers bookmarking docs ·
Designers building moodboards · Anyone who reads and watches a lot.

Start building your second brain today. It's free.
```
