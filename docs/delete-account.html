Delete your KeepIt account

How to delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your KeepIt account (developer: Keshab Sarkar) and the data associated with it.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open KeepIt and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and all cloud data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Email us from the Google address you signed in with and we'll delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email.

Request deletion by email

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase account (name, email, profile photo, user ID)
  • All saved items stored in the cloud (links, titles, descriptions, tags, status)
  • Sync metadata

What is kept

Nothing is retained on our side after deletion. Items stored locally on your phone are removed when you delete your account in the app, or when you uninstall the app. Google may keep standard security logs for a limited time under its own policies.

Using KeepIt without an account? Your data only exists on your phone. Go to Profile → Delete all local data, or uninstall the app.
